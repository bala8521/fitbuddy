# Phase 7: Project Documentation - User Manual & Operating Guide

## 1. Getting Started with FitBuddy

Welcome to FitBuddy! Follow this step-by-step guide to generate, customize, and manage your AI-powered 7-day fitness and nutrition program.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step User Instructions

### Step 1: Onboarding & Profile Creation
1. Navigate to the FitBuddy homepage ([http://127.0.0.1:8000](http://127.0.0.1:8000)).
2. Click the vibrant **"Get Started"** or **"Generate Your Routine"** button.
3. Fill in your personal fitness parameters:
   - **Name**: Your preferred display name.
   - **Age**: Your current age (between 14 and 100).
   - **Gender**: Select Male, Female, or Non-Binary.
   - **Weight (kg)**: Enter your current weight in kilograms.
   - **Primary Goal**: Choose from *Weight Loss*, *Muscle Gain*, or *General Wellness*.
   - **Workout Intensity**: Select *Low*, *Medium*, or *High*.
   - **Experience Level**: Choose *Beginner*, *Intermediate*, or *Advanced*.
4. Click **"Generate My Workout Plan"**.

---

### Step 2: Exploring Your 7-Day Microcycle Schedule
1. Once generated, your 7-day interactive workout dashboard appears.
2. Click across the **Day 1** through **Day 7** tabs to view:
   - **Daily Focus**: Target muscle group or aerobic objective.
   - **Dynamic Warmup**: 5–8 minute mobility drills.
   - **Primary Exercise Scheme**: Exercise names, target muscles, set counts, repetition ranges, rest timers, and form cues.
   - **Cool-Down & Recovery**: Static stretches and recovery protocols.

---

### Step 3: Refining Your Workout Plan (Adaptive Feedback)
1. If you need modifications (e.g., you prefer bodyweight exercises, need extra rest, or have sore shoulders), click **"Refine Plan"**.
2. Type your plain-English adjustment into the feedback field:
   - *Example*: *"Please replace barbell squats with dumbbell lunges and make Wednesday a pure core day."*
3. Click **"Submit Revision"**.
4. FitBuddy's AI remodels your 7-day routine and updates the schedule, advancing the version tag (e.g., `v1.0 -> v2.0`).

---

### Step 4: Accessing Nutrition & Recovery Guidance
1. Click **"Nutrition & Recovery"** in the top navigation bar.
2. View science-backed nutritional advice customized to your primary goal:
   - Daily calorie target guidelines
   - Protein distribution & pacing
   - Pre-workout and post-workout meal timing
   - Daily hydration benchmarks & electrolyte rules
   - Sleep hygiene & circadian recovery protocols.
