# Phase 1: Brainstorming & Ideation - Target Audience & User Personas

## 1. Target Audience Segmentation

FitBuddy caters to a wide spectrum of health-conscious individuals, categorized into three distinct market segments:

1. **Fitness Beginners & Wellness Seekers**: Individuals starting their fitness journey seeking structured, injury-free, easy-to-follow guidance.
2. **Intermediate Gym Goers & Hypertrophy Enthusiasts**: Lifters aiming to break plateaus, optimize progressive overload, and structure specific body part splits.
3. **Busy Working Professionals**: Time-constrained individuals who need high-efficiency workouts, quick meal strategies, and adaptive routines.

---

## 2. Detailed User Personas

### Persona 1: Sarah Jenkins – The Motivated Beginner
- **Age**: 28
- **Occupation**: Digital Marketing Specialist
- **Fitness Goal**: Weight Loss & General Wellness
- **Intensity Level**: Medium
- **Experience**: Beginner
- **Frustrations**: Overwhelmed by gym equipment, intimidated by complex workout spreadsheets, frequently gets sore and abandons routines.
- **FitBuddy Needs**: Clear exercise form cues, dynamic warmups, structured rest periods, and simple nutrition advice without complicated calorie tracking.

### Persona 2: Alex Rivera – The Dedicated Lifter
- **Age**: 24
- **Occupation**: Software Engineer
- **Fitness Goal**: Muscle Gain & Strength Hypertrophy
- **Intensity Level**: High
- **Experience**: Intermediate
- **Frustrations**: Repetitive training routines, lack of variation in hypertrophy splits, plateauing on compound lifts.
- **FitBuddy Needs**: 7-day push/pull/legs or upper/lower variations, precise rep-in-reserve (RIR) guidelines, optimal protein intake timing, and ability to request upper-body emphasis via feedback.

### Persona 3: Marcus Vance – The Executive on the Move
- **Age**: 42
- **Occupation**: Operations Director
- **Fitness Goal**: General Wellness & Cardiovascular Health
- **Intensity Level**: Low to Medium
- **Experience**: Intermediate
- **Frustrations**: Irregular schedule, frequent business travel, high stress, poor sleep.
- **FitBuddy Needs**: Fast routine generation, recovery and sleep hygiene tips, easy adjustments (e.g., "Change Wednesday to an active recovery walk").

## 3. Empathy Map Summary

| User Says | User Thinks | User Does | User Feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| "I want a clear workout plan that fits my schedule." | "Will I be able to stick with this without burning out?" | Searches online fitness blogs, gets conflicting advice. | Intimidated, motivated yet skeptical of generic routines. |
| "I need to adjust this routine because my knees hurt today." | "I hope I don't have to start from scratch." | Manually skips exercises or stops working out. | Frustrated when tools are rigid and non-adaptive. |
| "What should I eat to support this training?" | "I just need simple, actionable nutrition rules." | Spends hours researching fad diets. | Overwhelmed by excessive dietary information. |
